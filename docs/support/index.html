Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@brads.io. Tell us which app, your device and iOS version, and what happened.

Common questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app’s Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads once in the app’s Settings to turn off pop-up and banner ads for good, or start the monthly subscription, which turns off all ads and unlocks everything while it is active.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, then choose the app.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress isn’t on my new device.

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud is on for the app (Settings › [your name] › iCloud). Open the app on the old device once while online so it can sync.
