Stitchwell privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers Stitchwell for iPhone and iPad, published by Yucel Sanli.

The short version: we do not collect any data about you. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”.

What we collect

Nothing. Our apps do not send personal information, usage data, identifiers or diagnostics to us or to anyone else. We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share data.

Where your game data lives

On your device

Your progress, settings and creations are stored on your device.

Your own iCloud (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app can sync your progress between your devices using Apple’s iCloud in your private iCloud account. We cannot see or access this data. You can turn this off in your device’s Settings › [your name] › iCloud, and delete it there.

Photos you choose

The app lets you turn a photo into a puzzle. You pick the photo with Apple’s photo picker, so the app only sees the photo you choose and never your whole library. The photo is processed on your device and is never uploaded. Only the finished pattern is saved, on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether a purchase is active. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Game Center (optional)

If you choose to use Game Center for achievements or leaderboards, Apple handles it under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We only see what Apple shows to all developers on a leaderboard, such as your Game Center nickname and score.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are off until you turn them on.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Our apps are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Data in your iCloud account is under your control. If you have a question, write to us and we will answer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If an app ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released.
