Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers every app published by Yucel Sanli (“we”) on the App Store (“our apps”). Each app also has its own page, listed at the bottom.

The short version: Our apps collect no data about you. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no accounts. Your progress stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud. Purchases and Game Center are handled by Apple.

No ads, no tracking

The current version of our apps has no ads and contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting software. It never asks to track you and never reads the advertising identifier. We do not sell or share your personal information, because we do not collect any.

Not in the current version: a later update may add ads. If it does, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released, describe exactly which data the ad provider collects, and explain your choices (including the tracking prompt and a “Do Not Sell or Share” option).

Data we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

Where your game data lives

On your device

Your progress, settings and creations are stored on your device.

Your own iCloud (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, each app’s progress can sync between your devices through Apple’s iCloud, in your private iCloud account. We cannot see or access this data. You can turn this off and delete it in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Photos you choose

Some of our apps let you turn a photo into a puzzle. You pick the photo with Apple’s photo picker, so the app only sees the photo you choose and never your whole library. The photo is processed on your device and is never uploaded. Only the finished pattern is saved, on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether a purchase or subscription is active. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Game Center (optional)

Game Center is off until you turn it on in an app’s settings. If you do, Apple handles your achievements (and, in apps that have them, leaderboard scores) under Apple’s Privacy Policy. On a leaderboard we see only what Apple shows every developer, such as your nickname and score.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are off until you turn them on.

Diagnostics you choose to send

If an app crashes or freezes, iOS may give it a technical report (for example, which part of the code was running). The app keeps these reports on your device only. They leave it only if you choose to send them: that opens the share sheet, and you decide where they go, for example an email to us. They contain no photos, patterns or personal details.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Our apps are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to the use of personal data. We do not hold personal data about you, apart from emails you choose to send us. Write to us and we will answer any request, including deleting your emails.

Emails to support

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and keep them only as long as we need to answer and follow up. We never add you to a mailing list or share your email.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above, and update the App Store privacy label before the changed version is released.
