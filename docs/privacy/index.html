Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers every app published by Yucel Sanli (“we”) on the App Store (“our apps”). Each app also has its own page, listed at the bottom.

The short version: our apps are free to play with ads. The ads are provided by AppLovin, which collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads. If you allow tracking, ads can be personalised; if you don’t, you still see ads, just not personalised ones. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off pop-up and banner ads, and a monthly subscription removes all ads. We do not run our own analytics, and we have no accounts.

Data collected for advertising

When you use the free version, the advertising software inside the app (AppLovin, including its ad mediation partners) may collect:

  • Device identifiers — the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and identifiers for this app on your device;
  • Device and network information — such as device model, operating system, language, IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • Ad interactions and app usage — such as which ads were shown or tapped and basic events needed to decide when ads are shown;
  • Diagnostics — such as ad-loading errors and performance data.

This data is used to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you allow tracking, personalise them. AppLovin processes it under its own privacy policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/. We receive only aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown) and cannot identify you from them.

Your choices

  • Tracking: after your first finished game, the app asks whether it may track you (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt). You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Personalised ads: if you decline tracking, ads are not personalised.
  • Fewer or no ads: the one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off pop-up and banner ads; an active monthly subscription turns off all ads.
  • Opt-out requests: you can ask AppLovin not to sell or share your data, or to delete data it holds, using the options in its privacy policy.

Data we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. We do not use separate analytics, attribution or crash-reporting tools. We do not sell data ourselves.

Where your game data lives

On your device

Your progress, settings and creations are stored on your device.

Your own iCloud (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app can sync your progress between your devices using Apple’s iCloud in your private iCloud account. We cannot see or access this data. You can turn this off and delete it in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Photos you choose

Some of our apps let you turn a photo into a puzzle. You pick the photo with Apple’s photo picker, so the app only sees the photo you choose and never your whole library. The photo is processed on your device and is never uploaded or shared with advertisers. Only the finished pattern is saved, on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether a purchase or subscription is active. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Game Center (optional)

If you choose to use Game Center for achievements or leaderboards, Apple handles it under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We only see what Apple shows to all developers on a leaderboard, such as your Game Center nickname and score.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are off until you turn them on.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Our apps are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used one of our apps and data was collected, contact us and we will help you ask AppLovin to delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to the use of personal data. We do not hold personal data about you ourselves; advertising data is held by AppLovin, and you can use its privacy options or write to us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above, and update the App Store privacy label before the changed version is released.
